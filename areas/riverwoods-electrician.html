Electrical Work in Riverwoods Homes

Riverwoods is unlike anywhere else we work. Most of the village sits under a genuine oak canopy, with custom homes from the 1950s through the 1980s tucked back on acre lots, often barely visible from the road. It's beautiful — and it's also the most outage-prone corner of our service area. When a summer storm or an ice event rolls through, all those mature trees and overhead lines mean Riverwoods residents are frequently the first to lose power and among the last to get it back.

The housing stock adds its own wrinkles. Many of these homes were custom-built decades ago with panels sized for a different era, and we still find Federal Pacific equipment and aging fuse boxes behind utility-room doors. Long setbacks mean long feeder runs, and detached garages, pools, and outbuildings that were added over the years aren't always wired the way they should be. A fair number of Riverwoods homes also rely on private wells and sump systems, which turns every outage into a plumbing problem too.

That combination — deep lots, heavy canopy, older custom wiring, and equipment that genuinely needs power to protect the house — is why standby generators, surge protection, and outdoor power are the jobs Riverwoods homeowners call us for most.

Common Electrical Problems We See in Riverwoods

  • Extended power outages after storms. Riverwoods has the heaviest tree cover in our service area, and limbs on overhead lines mean outages here tend to hit harder and last longer than in neighboring towns.
  • Sump and well equipment dead in an outage. Many homes here depend on sump pumps — and some on private wells — so losing power can mean a flooded basement or no water until the lines are restored.
  • Voltage spikes when power comes back. The flicker-and-surge cycle of storm restorations is rough on well pump motors, sump pumps, furnace boards, and modern appliances, and we see the damage afterward.
  • Aging panels in 1950s–1980s custom homes. We still find Federal Pacific panels and undersized services in Riverwoods, often carrying loads the original electrician never planned for.
  • Dark, unlit driveways and grounds. With long setbacks and deep shade, failed or never-installed landscape and driveway lighting is a real safety issue, not just a curb-appeal one.

Our Most-Requested Services in Riverwoods

These are the four jobs Riverwoods homeowners ask us for most, shaped by the village's canopy, big lots, and outage history. Every service call also includes a free once-over of your panel and wiring, so you know where your system stands.

  • Generator Installation & Maintenance — With this much canopy over the lines, a whole-home standby generator is the single most requested job we do in Riverwoods — sized right, permitted, and maintained so it starts when the trees come down.
  • Outdoor Lighting — Long driveways and deep-set homes make driveway, path, and landscape lighting a genuine safety upgrade here, and we design it to work with the trees, not against them.
  • Whole-Home Surge Protection — A whole-home surge protector at the panel shields well pumps, sump pumps, HVAC boards, and electronics from the voltage spikes that come with storm restorations.
  • Dedicated Circuits — Sump systems, well equipment, workshops, and home offices each get their own circuit, so one tripped breaker never takes down the gear your house actually depends on.
