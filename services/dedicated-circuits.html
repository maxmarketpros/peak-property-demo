Dedicated Circuits in Skokie & the North Shore

One appliance, one breaker. We run dedicated circuits for homes across Skokie and the North Shore so your sump pump, space heater, or kitchen gear stops fighting for power.

If your microwave and toaster can't run at the same time, or the breaker pops every time the space heater kicks on, the fix is usually simple. That appliance needs its own circuit. One line from the panel, one breaker, no sharing.

A lot of houses around Skokie, Evanston, and Wilmette were wired decades before anyone owned an air fryer, a sump pump, and two computers in a home office. The old circuits are stretched thin. Overloaded shared circuits don't just trip. They run hot, and heat is how wiring trouble starts.

Signs an Appliance Needs Its Own Circuit

  • The breaker trips when two kitchen appliances run at once
  • Lights dim when the sump pump, AC, or a big appliance kicks on
  • A space heater keeps knocking out power to half a room
  • An outlet or cord feels warm to the touch after heavy use
  • You're running a garage workshop or home office off one overloaded line
  • The manual for your new appliance says "dedicated circuit required" and you don't have one
  • You rely on medical equipment and can't risk a nuisance trip

What's Included

  • A load check on your panel to confirm it has room for the new circuit
  • A new breaker sized correctly for the appliance it serves
  • A dedicated line run from the panel to exactly where you need it
  • The right outlet for the job, including GFCI or 240V where code calls for it
  • Clean routing through basements, walls, or garages with minimal disruption
  • Testing under real load before we call it done
  • Our free once-over of your panel and visible wiring, with photos of anything unsafe

What Does a Dedicated Circuit Cost in the Skokie Area?

It depends on a few things. How far the new line has to travel from the panel to the outlet. Whether the appliance needs a standard 120V circuit or a heavier 240V line. How easy the route is, since an unfinished basement run is quicker than fishing wire through finished plaster walls. And whether your panel has open slots or needs work first, which matters in older homes still running fuse boxes or Federal Pacific panels.
