Electrical Safety Inspections in Skokie & the North Shore

A licensed electrician goes through your whole home, panel to outlets, and shows you photos of anything unsafe. Serving Skokie and the North Shore for 18+ years.

Most houses around Skokie and the North Shore went up between the 1920s and the 1970s. That means fuse boxes, Federal Pacific panels, and fifty years of previous owners' handiwork hiding behind the walls. Some of it is fine. Some of it isn't. The trouble is you can't tell from the living room.

Johnny and our crew have spent 18+ years opening panels in these exact houses. An inspection with us is simple. We check everything, photograph what we find, and tell you the truth. If your electrical is in good shape, we say so and shake your hand. No scare tactics, no pressure, ever.

When It's Smart to Get Your Home Inspected

  • You're buying or selling a home and want a real electrician's opinion, not just a home inspector's glance
  • Your house was built before 1980 and the panel has never been replaced
  • There's a Federal Pacific label on your panel, or you still have a fuse box
  • Breakers trip often, lights flicker, or outlets feel warm to the touch
  • A previous owner "did some wiring" and you have no idea what's back there
  • You just had a scare: a burning smell, a spark, a buzzing panel

What's Included

  • Full panel check: brand, age, double-tapped breakers, signs of overheating or corrosion
  • Visible wiring review in the basement, attic, and utility areas
  • Outlet testing for grounding, polarity, and GFCI protection where code calls for it
  • A hunt for DIY junctions, open splices, and undersized wire from past owners
  • Photos of every issue, so you see exactly what we see
  • Written findings with honest recommendations, ranked by what actually matters
  • Straight answers on what's urgent, what can wait, and what's fine as-is
