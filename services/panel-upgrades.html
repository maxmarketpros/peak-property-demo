Panel Upgrades in Skokie & the North Shore

A lot of homes around Skokie and the North Shore still run on panels from another era. Johnny and the crew replace them week in, week out, and we'll tell you straight whether yours needs it.

Your electrical panel is the heart of the house. Every light, every outlet, every appliance runs through it. When it's undersized or failing, you feel it: tripped breakers, flickering lights, a fuse box you're scared to touch.

Around here the housing stock is old, and so are a lot of the panels. We've pulled plenty of Federal Pacific panels and original fuse boxes out of homes built between the 1920s and 1970s. Some insurance companies won't even write a policy until those are gone.

We're a small family crew, licensed and insured (IL #SE8133), with 18+ years doing this work. On every service call we give your panel a free once-over and show you photos of anything unsafe. No scare tactics. Just what we found and what it means.

Signs Your Panel Is Due for an Upgrade

  • Breakers trip whenever you run the microwave and the AC at the same time
  • The panel buzzes, hums, or feels warm to the touch
  • You still have a fuse box, or a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel
  • Your insurance company or home inspector flagged the panel
  • You want an EV charger, central air, or a kitchen remodel and there's no room for new circuits
  • Lights dim when big appliances kick on
  • Rust, scorch marks, or a burning smell near the panel

What's Included

  • Full replacement of Federal Pacific, Zinsco, and fuse-box panels with modern breaker panels
  • 100-amp to 200-amp service upgrades, and larger when the house calls for it
  • New breakers, grounding, and bonding brought up to current code
  • Permits and village inspection handled for you
  • Photos of the old panel and wiring so you can see exactly what we replaced
  • Clear labeling of every circuit before we leave
  • Panel work sized for future loads like EV chargers and AC

How We Work

Panel work follows the same four steps as everything we do. You call, we come look, and you get a free written quote with photos of what we found. On install day the power is typically off for part of the day, and we walk you through the new panel before we go. If something urgent turns up mid-job, we tell you first, not after.

  • 1. Free estimate & safety check — we look at the job, give your panel a free once-over, and quote upfront pricing.

  • 4. Final walkthrough — we test everything, clean up, and walk you through what we did.

What Does a Panel Upgrade Cost in the Skokie Area?

Honest answer: it depends on the house. The biggest factors are the size of the new service (a straight panel swap costs less than a 100-to-200 amp service upgrade), the condition of the wiring feeding the panel, and how much grounding and bonding work is needed to meet code. Panel location matters too. A panel in an open basement is quicker than one buried behind finished walls.

What we won't do is guess over the phone and surprise you later. Johnny or one of the crew will come out, look at the panel, and give you a free written quote before any work starts. The price you approve is the price you pay.
