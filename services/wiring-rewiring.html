Wiring & Rewiring in Skokie & the North Shore

Plenty of homes in Skokie and up the North Shore still run on wiring older than the people living in them. Johnny and the crew replace it the careful way, without tearing your house apart.

If your house went up before 1950, there is a decent chance some of the original wiring is still in the walls. Knob-and-tube. Cloth insulation gone brittle. Splices buried where nobody can see them. That wiring did fine for a radio and a few lamps. It was never meant for what a house runs today.

We are a small family crew out of Skokie, licensed and insured (IL license #SE8133), with 18+ years working on exactly this housing stock. On every visit we give your panel and visible wiring a free once-over, and if we spot something unsafe, we show you photos. No scare talk. Just what we found.

The part homeowners dread is the mess. Fair. Our answer: we fish new wire through finished walls wherever we can, so a rewire does not have to mean gutting your plaster.

Signs Your Home May Need Rewiring

  • Your home was built before 1950 and still has knob-and-tube or cloth-insulated wiring
  • Your insurance company is asking about old wiring before they will renew the policy
  • Two-prong outlets through the whole house, with nothing grounded
  • Lights dim when the fridge, microwave, or AC kicks on
  • Aluminum branch wiring from the 1960s or 70s, with warm outlets or flickering
  • You are remodeling a kitchen, bath, or basement and the old wiring cannot carry the new load

What's Included

  • Whole-home rewiring for older houses with knob-and-tube or cloth-covered wiring
  • Partial rewiring, one floor or a few circuits at a time if that fits your budget better
  • Aluminum branch wiring replacement or approved connection repairs
  • Fishing new copper through finished plaster and drywall with minimal openings
  • Grounded three-prong outlets and GFCI protection where code calls for it
  • Remodel wiring for kitchens, baths, and basement finishes
  • Documentation your insurance company can use
  • A free once-over of your panel and visible wiring on every visit

How We Work

A rewire runs through the same four steps as everything we do: you call, we come look, you get a free written quote, and work starts when you say go. On rewiring, the middle step matters most. We walk the whole house, figure out what is actually behind the walls, and price the job room by room so you can see where every dollar goes.

  • 1. Free estimate & safety check — we look at the job, give your panel a free once-over, and quote upfront pricing.
  • 2. Straightforward plan — scope, materials, timeline, and cost in plain English before we start.
  • 3. Professional execution — licensed electricians, clean work, everything to code.
  • 4. Final walkthrough — we test everything, clean up, and walk you through what we did.

What Does Rewiring a House Cost in the Skokie Area?

Honest answer: it depends on the house. The big factors are square footage and circuit count, what kind of old wiring is coming out (knob-and-tube takes more care than cloth-covered cable), how easy your walls and ceilings are to reach, and whether your panel can take the new circuits or needs upgrading too.

Replacing a few problem circuits is a very different job than a full rewire of a 1920s two-story, which is why we will not quote it over the phone. We come out, look at the actual house, and hand you a free written quote before any work starts. Call (773) 987-7677 and we will set it up.
