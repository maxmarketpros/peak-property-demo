Call Now, or Can It Wait Until Morning?

  • A burning or fishy smell near the panel, an outlet, or anywhere you can't place
  • Sparks, crackling, or buzzing coming from the panel or a switch
  • The whole house is dark but the neighbors still have power
  • A storm or falling tree took down the line to your house or bent the mast
  • An outlet or switch plate that's hot to the touch or shows scorch marks
  • Water getting into the panel or meter
  • A breaker that trips again the second you reset it, with any heat or smell

How We Work

Our usual four steps don't change at midnight, they just speed up. You call, we answer. We head out and diagnose on the spot. We make the danger safe first, then put the full repair in writing. And we don't leave until you know exactly what happened and what comes next.

  • 1. Free estimate & safety check — we look at the job, give your panel a free once-over, and quote upfront pricing.

What Does an Emergency Electrician Cost in Skokie and the North Shore?

Honest answer: it depends on what broke. Four things move the number most. The hour you call, since overnight and holiday visits run higher than daytime ones. What actually failed, because a burned breaker is a small job and a damaged service mast is not. Whether tonight's fix is a temporary make-safe or the full repair. And parts. Some old panels, Federal Pacific especially, can't be safely repaired at all and need replacement.

Here's what doesn't depend: before any repair starts, you get a written quote and a plain explanation of what we found. If the smart move is a safe patch tonight and the real work in daylight, we'll tell you that too. Estimates are free. Call (773) 987-7677.
