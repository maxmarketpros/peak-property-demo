How We Work

Every repair follows the same four steps: you call, we come out and diagnose, we give you a free written estimate, and then we do the work. For troubleshooting, that second step matters most. We don't touch a thing until we know the real cause, because replacing a breaker that keeps tripping without asking why it trips just hides the problem.

  • 1. Free estimate & safety check — we look at the job, give your panel a free once-over, and quote upfront pricing.
  • 2. Straightforward plan — scope, materials, timeline, and cost in plain English before we start.
  • 3. Professional execution — licensed electricians, clean work, everything to code.
  • 4. Final walkthrough — we test everything, clean up, and walk you through what we did.

What Does an Electrical Repair Cost in the Skokie Area?

Honest answer: it depends on what we find. A dead outlet with a loose connection is a quick fix. The same dead outlet caused by damaged wiring behind the wall is a bigger job. That's exactly why we diagnose before we quote anything.

A few things move the price: how hard the fault is to reach, the age and condition of your wiring (a lot of North Shore homes still have original circuits), whether the problem sits in the panel itself, and how many circuits are affected. Older fuse boxes and Federal Pacific panels sometimes turn a simple repair conversation into a replacement conversation, and we'll tell you straight if that's the case.

Either way, you get a free written quote before any work starts. You'll know the cause, see the photos, and know the price. Then you decide.
