Smoke & CO Detector Installation in Skokie & the North Shore

Working smoke and CO detectors are the cheapest insurance a house can have. We install and wire them right for families across Skokie and the North Shore.

Most homes we walk into have a detector problem. One is chirping in a hallway. One is twenty years old and painted over. One is missing entirely from the basement where the furnace lives. Nobody plans to fix these things until three in the morning, when the chirping starts.

We're Peak Property Electric, a family-owned crew based in Skokie. Johnny and the guys have spent 18+ years wiring homes around the North Shore, and detector work comes up on almost every call. Illinois updated its smoke detector law, plenty of older homes here still don't meet it, and hardwiring alarms is genuinely an electrician's job, not a ladder-and-screwdriver job.

We'll put the right detectors in the right rooms, tie them together so they all sound at once, and show you exactly what we did. Call (773) 987-7677 for a free estimate.

Signs Your Detectors Are Overdue

  • A detector keeps chirping even after you change the battery
  • Your smoke alarms are more than 10 years old (the date is printed on the back)
  • You still have battery-only detectors and haven't updated since Illinois changed its law
  • One alarm goes off but the others in the house stay silent
  • There's no CO detector within 15 feet of the bedrooms, or none near the gas furnace or water heater
  • A detector is yellowed, painted over, or dangling from its wires
  • You just bought an older home and honestly have no idea what's up there

What's Included

  • Hardwired smoke and CO detector installation, including new wiring where none exists
  • Interconnected alarms, so when one sounds, they all sound
  • 10-year sealed-battery units that meet current Illinois law
  • Replacement of expired, chirping, or nuisance-tripping detectors
  • Combination smoke/CO units where they make sense
  • Placement guidance: bedrooms, hallways, each level, near sleeping areas
  • Removal and disposal of your old units
  • A free once-over of your panel and visible wiring while we're there

Frequently Asked Questions

If your home relies on battery-powered smoke detectors, Illinois now requires 10-year sealed-battery units when they're replaced. Homes with hardwired detectors need to keep them hardwired. We'll tell you plainly which rule applies to your house and what it takes to comply.
Smoke detectors go in every bedroom, in the hallway outside sleeping areas, and on every level including the basement. Illinois requires a CO detector within 15 feet of every sleeping room in homes with gas appliances or an attached garage. We map it out room by room during the estimate.
When alarms are wired together, smoke in the basement sets off the alarm in your bedroom too. In a two- or three-story house, that head start matters. If your home was built before interconnection was standard, we can wire it in.
