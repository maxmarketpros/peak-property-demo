Whole-Home Surge Protection in Skokie & the North Shore

One device at your panel stands between the grid and everything you own that plugs in. We install whole-home surge protectors for families across Skokie and the North Shore.

Most people think surges come from lightning. Some do. But most ride in on the utility lines, small jolts from grid switching that hit your house every week. Each one chips away at circuit boards. Then one big hit finishes the job, and the fridge, the furnace board, and the TV all die on the same afternoon.

A power strip can't stop that. It only guards what's plugged into it, and cheap ones quit working without telling you. A whole-home surge protector mounts at your electrical panel and clamps the surge before it reaches a single outlet. That covers the things no strip can reach: your AC's control board, your EV charger, the oven, the sump pump.
