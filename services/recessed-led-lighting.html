Recessed & LED Lighting in Skokie & the North Shore

Clean holes, straight rows, no flicker. Johnny and the crew install recessed and LED lighting in Skokie and North Shore homes, and we leave your ceiling looking like we were never there.

<p>A lot of houses around here were built long before anyone thought about ceiling lighting. One bulb in the middle of the room, or a floor lamp in every corner. Recessed LED lighting fixes that without eating your headroom, and it changes how a room feels more than almost anything else you can do for the money.</p><p>The catch is the ceiling. Cutting into finished drywall or old plaster takes a careful hand, and fishing wire through closed ceilings is where sloppy work shows up later. We are a small family crew. Johnny has done this for 18 years, and our guys cut clean, keep the dust down, and line the rows up straight.</p><p>Basements, kitchens, whole floors. We plan the layout with you first, then do the work in one visit whenever we can. <a href="tel:7739877677">Call (773) 987-7677</a> for a free estimate.</p>

Signs Your Home Is Ready for Recessed Lighting

  • One ceiling fixture is doing all the work, and the corners of the room stay dark
  • You are running the kitchen off a lamp and the light over the stove
  • The basement feels like a basement, even after you finished it
  • Old can lights that run hot, burn out bulbs, or yellow with age
  • Bulbs flicker or buzz when you dim them
  • You are repainting or remodeling anyway and want the lighting done right first
  • Every room in the house is a slightly different shade of white light

What's Included

  • Recessed can and canless (wafer) LED installs in finished or open ceilings
  • LED retrofits of older can lights, so you keep the holes and lose the heat and bulb changes
  • Whole-room and whole-floor lighting layouts, planned with you before we cut
  • Kitchen and basement lighting, including damp-rated fixtures where they belong
  • LED-compatible dimmer switches, matched to the fixtures so nothing flickers
  • Color temperature guidance, from warm 2700K bedrooms to bright 4000K workspaces
  • Clean, precise cuts in drywall and plaster, with dust control and full cleanup
  • New circuits or switch legs run from the panel when the job needs them

What Does Recessed Lighting Cost in the Skokie Area?

It depends on a few things. How many lights you want. Whether your ceiling is open or finished, since fishing wire through drywall and plaster takes more time than wiring a bare joist bay. Whether we can tie into an existing circuit or need to run a new one from the panel. And what you pick: basic canless wafers, adjustable-color-temperature fixtures, and smart dimmers all price differently.

Here is how we handle it. We come out, look at your ceiling, count the lights with you, and give you a free written quote before any work starts. The number we put on paper is the number you pay. If we open a ceiling and find something unexpected, we talk to you first, not after.
