Outlets & Switches in Skokie & the North Shore

Dead outlets, loose plugs, switches that crackle. We fix them every week in Skokie and up the North Shore, and no job is too small to call about.

An outlet that quit working is easy to ignore. You plug the lamp in somewhere else and move on. But in the older homes around here, a dead or loose outlet is often the first sign of a worn connection behind the wall, and that is worth twenty minutes of an electrician's time.

Peak Property Electric is a small family crew out of Skokie. Johnny and the guys have spent 18+ years working on the outlets, switches, and old ungrounded wiring in this area's 1920s-1970s houses. We show up in uniform, fix what you called about, and give your panel and visible wiring a free once-over while we're there. If we spot something unsafe, you get photos, not a lecture.

Need one GFCI swapped in the bathroom? Call. Small jobs are how most of our customers found us.

Signs an Outlet or Switch Needs Attention

  • An outlet is dead, or only works if you wiggle the plug
  • Plugs slide out of the outlet under their own weight
  • An outlet or switch plate feels warm or looks browned
  • You still have two-prong outlets that won't take a grounded cord
  • A GFCI or breaker trips every time you run the hair dryer or microwave
  • A switch crackles, buzzes, or the lights flicker when you flip it
  • No GFCI protection near the kitchen sink, in the bathroom, garage, or outside

What's Included

  • GFCI outlets installed in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, and laundry areas
  • Dead, loose, or damaged outlets and switches replaced
  • Two-prong outlets upgraded to grounded three-prong
  • USB and USB-C combo outlets so chargers stop hogging plugs
  • Dimmer switches, including ones that play nice with LED bulbs
  • Weatherproof exterior outlets for holiday lights and yard tools
  • New outlets added where you actually need them
  • A free once-over of your panel and visible wiring on every visit

What Does Outlet or Switch Work Cost in the Skokie Area?

It depends on a few things. How many outlets or switches we're replacing, since doing five at once costs far less per device than five separate trips. Whether the wiring behind the box is grounded, because two-prong upgrades in older homes sometimes need a ground path added. And the type of device, since a GFCI or smart dimmer costs more than a standard outlet.

What you'll never get from us is a surprise. You get a free written quote before any work starts, and the price we quote is the price you pay. If we open a box and find something worse, we stop, show you photos, and let you decide.
