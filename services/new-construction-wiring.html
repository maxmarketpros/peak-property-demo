Projects Where You Need a New-Construction Electrician

  • You are building a new home and the GC needs a licensed electrical sub who shows up on schedule
  • You are adding a room, dormer, or second story that needs its own circuits
  • You are gutting a 1920s-1970s house to the studs and the old wiring should not go back in
  • You are finishing a basement and want lighting, outlets, and future capacity roughed in properly
  • Your renovation plans call for more power than the existing panel or service can deliver
  • An electrical inspection failed or stalled and you need someone to get the job back on track

What's Included

⚡ While We're There: The Free Panel Check

How We Work

Our process does not change because the job is bigger. We look first, in this case at your plans and the site. We explain what we found and quote it in writing before work starts. Then we do the work in the right order, rough-in through trim-out, and walk you through everything before final inspection. On every visit we also give the panel and any visible wiring a free once-over, and if we spot something unsafe, you see photos of it, not just our word.

  • 1. Free estimate & safety check — we look at the job, give your panel a free once-over, and quote upfront pricing.
  • 2. Straightforward plan — scope, materials, timeline, and cost in plain English before we start.
  • 3. Professional execution — licensed electricians, clean work, everything to code.
  • 4. Final walkthrough — we test everything, clean up, and walk you through what we did.

What Does New Construction Wiring Cost in the Skokie Area?

No two builds price the same, so an honest answer starts with your plans. The big drivers are square footage and circuit count, the service size the house needs (a home with an EV charger and electric appliances needs more than one from 1965 did), and the finish schedule, since recessed lighting throughout costs more to wire than basic fixtures. Gut renovations add one more variable: what we find behind the old walls.

What we can promise is a free written quote before any work begins. We review your plans, walk the site, and put a real number on paper. Call (773) 987-7677 and we will take a look.
